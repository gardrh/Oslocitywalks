About LOSlo

LOSlo is a self-guided walking tour designed to help you discover the hidden stories and remarkable people behind Oslo's streets, squares, and buildings.

Each tour is carefully researched and built to be completed on foot. You'll encounter history, architecture, art, and local culture — entirely at your own pace.

Tours are regularly updated and new routes are in development. If you have suggestions or spot an error, we'd love to hear from you.

Have a great walk! 🚶
