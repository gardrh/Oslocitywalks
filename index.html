Self-guided walking tours

Discover Oslo on foot

Welcome to Los!, your guide to the people and places of Oslo. Los! navigates you through the streets of the city. You will be given riddles and clues in different places, and your job is to find the answer.

Tips & Tricks

  • The locations where you will find the answers are shown in bold. If you are not familiar with the city, enter these places into your preferred online maps service.
  • The duration of the tours is indicated, but may depend on your walking pace and possible stops along the way. We have made some recommendations on cafés and similar stops that might interest you.
  • At the end of the tour, we will show you how much time you spent. If you are part of a group, why not make it a competition!
