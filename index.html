Self-guided walking tours

Discover Oslo on foot

Welcome to Los! — your guide to the people and places of Oslo. You will be given riddles and clues at different locations across the city, and your job is to find the answers.

Tips & Tricks

  • Locations where you'll find the answers are shown in bold. If unfamiliar with the city, enter these into your maps app.
  • Tour duration is approximate and may vary based on your walking pace and any stops along the way.
  • At the end of the tour, we will show you how much time you spent. If you are part of a group, why not make it a competition!
